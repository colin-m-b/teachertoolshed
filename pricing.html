Why is history a paid feature?

Because a browser's local storage is reliable for a lesson and unreliable for a year. Safari clears it after a week idle, school laptops are reimaged over the summer, and a district policy push can wipe it without warning. A "term view" is only an honest promise once it comes with a way to keep the data alive across devices — which is what Pro is paying for, and what the Drive and OneDrive sync will finish.

What does buying Pro tell you about me?

The payment provider (the merchant of record) knows your name, email, card and billing country, and issues a licence key. When you paste the key into Class Lists, your browser sends only the key to the provider to check it is real, then keeps the answer locally and re-checks it about once a month. There is no account, no password and no login. We never see a roster.

What if my school blocks the licence check?

A key that has been checked once keeps working offline for 90 days. If it cannot be re-checked in that time it falls back to Free — and every record you made is still there, still exportable.

Can I just edit the page and unlock it?

Yes. The check runs in your browser and a determined person can flip it. We are not going to build a server to stop a teacher paying $3 a month from doing that, because the server would need accounts, and accounts are where student data starts leaking.
