Seating Chart Maker

What it is for, the first task, and the things that go wrong. Every screenshot and video uses the sample class — no real student appears anywhere.

What it is for

The Seating Chart Maker draws your room and puts students in it. Choose rows, islands or a circle, set the size, and Randomize fills the desks; drag a name onto another to swap, or use Swap Names for tap-tap swapping on a touch screen. Desk numbers count from the front so a substitute can read the chart without knowing the room. Print gives a clean sheet with the teacher's desk at the bottom. Charts can be saved and reloaded, and the work in progress is kept automatically between visits. With Pro, constraints — who sits in the front row, which pairs are never adjacent — are honoured by Randomize, and the chart downloads as a PDF.

The first task

  1. Pick a class from the dropdown, or paste names in. The count badge shows how many you have.
  2. Choose an arrangement: Rows (set columns, let rows fit automatically), Islands (set the size of each), or Circle.
  3. Press Randomize Seats. Press it again for a different draw. Drag a name onto another desk to swap the two.
  4. Press Print in the header. To keep the chart, press Save chart and name it.

Things that go wrong

Names are cut off on the desk.
Long names wrap to two lines; very long ones are trimmed with an ellipsis on screen but print in full on the sheet.
There are more students than desks.
With a fixed row count the tool adds rows at the back rather than dropping anyone, and says so.
I want two students apart every time.
That is a Pro constraint: type the pair in Keep apart, one per line, and Randomize keeps them from sitting side by side, in front of, or behind each other. Front row works the same way.
Download PDF is asking for Pro.
Print is always free: choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog and you get the same sheet.
It's cramped on my phone.
Dragging desks needs room. Use a laptop or a tablet in landscape.
