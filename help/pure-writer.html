Pure Writer

What it is for, the first task, and the things that go wrong. Every screenshot and video uses the sample class — no real student appears anywhere.

What it is for

Pure Writer is a writing page with nothing helping. You build a task — prompt, optional passage, time limit, word target — and share a link. A student opens it, types their name and ID, and writes in a plain box with spellcheck off, autocorrect off and pasting from outside blocked. When they finish they download an MLA-formatted PDF or Word file with their name, your name, the class and the date already in the header, and that is what they hand in. Their draft is saved in their own browser as they type, so a closed tab is not a lost essay. What you get is the writing as it actually is, which is the point.

The first task

  1. Fill in the task name, essay title, class, your name and the prompt. Add a stimulus passage if the task has one; it appears beside the writing box.
  2. Set minutes and a word target if you want them, and check the writing conditions: paste blocked, spellcheck off, and whether the box locks when time runs out.
  3. Press Generate link and share it. Press Print for a sheet with the prompt and the link's QR-free text for students who need it on paper.
  4. Open the link as a student: enter a name and ID, write, press Finish and export, and download the PDF. Check the header is right before the real lesson.

Things that go wrong

A student closed the tab.
Reopen the link on the same device and browser, enter the same name and ID, and the draft is back.
Autocorrect still changed a word.
The page turns off the browser's own checking and most extensions, but a device-level keyboard (a tablet's, say) can still correct. Turn that off in the device settings for exam conditions.
The PDF shows a box instead of a letter.
The PDF font covers Latin scripts fully but not every symbol. The tool warns when it hits one; download Word instead, which handles anything.
Can I see how long they took, or whether they left the tab?
Yes, with Pro's integrity cover page: a final page listing time on task, word count, tab focus losses and blocked paste attempts. The counts are always kept; Pro prints them.
I want to reuse the task next year.
Press Save this task at the top of the setup page. Free keeps one; Pro keeps a library.
