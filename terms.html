Using the free tools

Teacher Toolshed is a set of web pages that run in your browser. You may use them for teaching, at any school, for as long as you like, at no charge. They are provided as they are: they are built carefully and used daily by their author, but there is no warranty that they suit your purpose, and no liability for a lesson that goes sideways or a chart that prints crooked.

Everything you enter stays in your browser. The privacy page is the whole story; these terms do not change it.

The site is written by one teacher. It can change or stop at any time. Because your data is only ever in your browser, that does not affect your data: export a backup from Class Lists and you have everything.

A Pro licence

A Pro licence is a proof of purchase, not an account. It is a key that switches on the Pro features described on the Pro page in the browsers where you enter it.

  • Per teacher. An individual licence is for one teacher, on up to three devices at a time. It is not transferable. Sharing a key with a colleague is not licensed; buying a department key is.
  • Department and school licences cover the named number of teachers at one school site. The school may reassign seats as staff change.
  • Term. A licence runs for the period bought (a month or a year) and renews until cancelled. Cancel any time; Pro stays on until the paid period ends. Prices for existing subscribers are never raised mid-subscription.
  • Refunds. Thirty days from a purchase, on request, no questions. After that, no refunds for a partial period.
  • What ends with the licence. Only the Pro features. Every record you made stays in your browser, stays exportable, and shows again if you renew. Nothing is ever deleted by a licence lapsing.
  • What we can do. Disable a key that was obtained fraudulently or is being shared far beyond its seats. We will write first.
